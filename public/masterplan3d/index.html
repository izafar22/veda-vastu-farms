The plot grid, dimensions, road-width labels, feature labels and underlying masterplan come from the supplied AutoCAD PDF. Exact source annotations are preserved; no synthetic width/length values are used. Plot meshes are derived from the PDF's plot-fill geometry. Premium ArchViz V8 keeps those approved source-derived layers intact while upgrading conceptual presentation: architectural sky/horizon treatment, richer PBR-like materials, a more detailed clubhouse/pool, a more refined entry composition, warm architectural lighting, and a Blender-ready GLB replacement layer. Optional GLB files only replace conceptual vertical assets; PDF-derived plots, roads, green-area footprints, entry location, labels, dimensions, camera controls and interaction behavior remain unchanged.
